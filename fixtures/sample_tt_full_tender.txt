SECTION 1: INSTRUCTIONS TO TENDERERS
1.1 SCOPE OF PROPOSALS
The Water and Sewerage Authority (hereinafter referred to as "the Authority" or "WASA") invites eligible firms to submit competitive tenders for the provision of comprehensive pest extermination, rodent control, and fumigation services across all Authority administrative buildings, water treatment plants, booster stations, and depots throughout Trinidad and Tobago.

1.2 CLOSING DATE AND TIME
Tenders must be uploaded via the WASA Oracle ERP e-Procurement Portal no later than 2:00 P.M. on Wednesday, August 26, 2026.
Late submissions will be rejected automatically by the electronic tendering system. No physical, emailed, or facsimile tenders will be entertained.

SECTION 2: MANDATORY PRE-TENDER LOGISTICS
2.1 PRE-BID CONFERENCE AND SITE VISIT
A mandatory Pre-Bid Meeting and Site Visit will be conducted commencing Tuesday, August 4, 2026 at 10:00 A.M.
Locations: Authority North, Central, South, and Tobago Operational Facilities.
Bidders are required to notify the Procurement Department of the full names, designations, and contact numbers of their official representatives at least two (2) business days prior to the visit via email to procurement@agency.gov.tt.
Attendance is mandatory. Bidders who fail to attend and obtain a signed Certificate of Site Visit will have their tenders deemed non-responsive and disqualified.

2.2 CLARIFICATIONS AND QUERIES
Requests for clarification must be submitted via the Oracle Portal no later than August 12, 2026.
The Authority will issue formal responses to all queries by August 17, 2026.

SECTION 3: ELIGIBILITY AND STATUTORY COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS
Under the Public Procurement and Disposal of Public Property Act of Trinidad and Tobago, all tenderers must submit evidence of current statutory compliance:

3.1 TAX COMPLIANCE (BIR)
Tenderers must submit an original or certified copy of a valid Tax Clearance Certificate issued by the Board of Inland Revenue (BIR) of Trinidad and Tobago. The certificate must be in the legal name of the tendering entity.

3.2 VALUE ADDED TAX (VAT)
Tenderers must provide a valid Value Added Tax (VAT) Clearance Certificate from the Inland Revenue Division. Where a tenderer is exempt from VAT registration, an official letter of confirmation of non-registration issued by the Board of Inland Revenue must be supplied.

3.3 NATIONAL INSURANCE (NIS)
Tenderers must provide a valid Certificate of Compliance from the National Insurance Board of Trinidad and Tobago (NIBTT) confirming that all statutory employer and employee contributions are up to date.

SECTION 4: CORPORATE AND FINANCIAL CAPABILITY
4.1 CORPORATE REGISTRATION
Tenderers must provide:
(a) Certificate of Incorporation or Certificate of Continuance under the Companies Act of Trinidad and Tobago.
(b) Current Notice of Directors (Form 4 or Form 28) filed with the Registrar General.
(c) Company Profile indicating organizational structure and executive ownership.

4.2 FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
Tenderers must provide Audited Financial Statements (including Balance Sheet, Income Statement, and Notes) for the last three (3) consecutive financial years (2023, 2024, 2025). The statements must be certified by an independent practicing Chartered Accountant.

4.3 BID SECURITY / TENDER BOND
Each tender must be accompanied by a Tender Bond or Bid Security in the sum of TT$50,000.00 from a commercial bank or licensed insurance company operating in Trinidad and Tobago. The bond must remain valid for a period of one hundred and twenty (120) days beyond the tender closing date.

SECTION 5: TECHNICAL QUALIFICATIONS AND SAFETY
5.1 PAST PERFORMANCE AND EXPERIENCE
Tenderers must demonstrate a minimum of five (5) years of continuous commercial pest control experience. Submissions must include at least three (3) client reference letters confirming successful contract execution of similar magnitude in commercial or industrial settings within the last 5 years.

5.2 KEY PERSONNEL AND CERTIFICATIONS
Tenderers must submit detailed Curriculum Vitae (CVs) for the following personnel:
- Project Manager / Contract Lead (minimum 5 years supervisory experience)
- Certified Pest Control Technicians (holding valid Ministry of Health pesticide applicator certification)
- Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) Officer

5.3 HEALTH, SAFETY AND ENVIRONMENT (HSE) POLICY
Tenderers must submit their formal Company HSE / OSH Policy Statement in compliance with the Occupational Safety and Health Act (OSHA) of Trinidad and Tobago, along with safety data sheets (SDS) for all proposed chemicals.
